Red zone tracker

Rules in use (prototype defaults, a clinician sets them per patient)

  • Weight: amber at +1.0 kg in 2 days or +1.5 kg in 7 days; red at +2.0 kg in 3 days or +2.5 kg in 7 days. The 2 kg in 3 days rule follows the ESC Heart Failure Association patient advice.
  • Blood pressure: red at 180/110 or higher, or top number under 90; amber at 160/100 or higher, under 100, or 140/90 or higher three readings in a row.
  • Heart rate (resting): red above 120 or under 45; amber above 100 or under 50. No guideline source found: clinician-set.
  • Coffee: a yes adds a "recheck in 30 minutes" note to amber BP and heart rate. It never lowers a red zone.
  • Symptoms or no diary: a reported warning symptom is red. Two days without a diary is amber.

Synthetic patients, except the row marked LIVE, which reads the diary saved in this browser by the patient app. Not medical advice.
