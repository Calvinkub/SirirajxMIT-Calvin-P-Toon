| **Heart patients are told to exercise. Nobody tells them how.** | 0:00–0:15 | 45 | about 35 words). We are [team]. We help people with heart disease exercise safely after diagnosis. Today they get a sentence of advice and go home. We built an agentic AI on InterSystems IRIS for Health that turns the doctor’s limits into small daily missions. |
| **Somchai has the data, but not the next step** | 0:15–1:00 | 102 | about 100 words). Meet Somchai. He is fifty-eight. He played tennis every weekend. Now he has high blood pressure, and his doctor says: keep exercising, but gently. Then he goes home. His watch is full of data, but nobody tells him what is safe tomorrow morning. This gap is common. In one survey, ninety-three percent of GPs prescribed exercise, but only fifteen percent wrote it down. And in one Thai hospital, only twelve and a half percent of bypass patients came back for outpatient rehab. Most patients are on their own. They need a plan that fits their limits and their life. |
| **The doctor sets the limits. Our agent turns them into missions.** | 1:00–1:15 | 41 | about 35 words). Our idea is simple. The doctor sets the limits once. Our agent turns them into one or two missions a day, on the phone and watch. And warning symptoms are caught by rules, never by an AI model. |
| **Live demo: tennis is out, badminton is in** | 1:15–1:55 | 104 | about 90 words). Switch to the live demo. Click preset 1. Here is Somchai’s message. On the right you see what the agent does. First, rules check for warning symptoms. Clear. It reads his signed limits from FHIR, and the guideline from IRIS Vector Search. Then it filters a hundred activities down to sixty-seven. Tennis is out, too intense. A nearest-neighbour match finds what is closest to tennis: badminton, bowling, table tennis. It finds groups near his home and asks him to choose. Click badminton, then two sessions. His week is saved as FHIR Tasks. If the live page fails, speak over this slide. |
| **Built on IRIS for Health, with safety by construction** | 1:55–2:20 | 63 | about 60 words). Under the hood, everything lives in InterSystems IRIS for Health. Limits, missions and symptoms are FHIR resources. SQL Builder feeds the rules engine. Vector Search grounds the model in the approved guideline. Models only choose from what the rules allow. And we measured it: picking the tool in one forward pass was eighteen times faster, on our synthetic test set. |
| **Insurers pay for recovery; patients can start for free** | 2:20–2:45 | 61 | about 60 words). Who pays? First, insurers. After a heart attack claim, a recovery benefit keeps members active, and we prove the value in a pilot with outcomes agreed up front. Second, patients: a free starter week, then Premium, around four hundred ninety-nine baht, which we will test. The AI itself costs about one to two dollars per patient a month. |
| **Somchai doesn’t need more data. He needs a safe next step.** | 2:45–3:00 | 41 | about 35 words). We are asking for three things: InterSystems mentorship to run fully on IRIS, a cardiologist to sign our limit library, and a small supervised pilot. Somchai doesn’t need more data. He needs a safe next step. Thank you. |
