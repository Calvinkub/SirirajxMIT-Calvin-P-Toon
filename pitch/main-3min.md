| **One guideline for everyone. No plan for Somchai.** | 0:15–1:00 | 101 | about 105 words). Meet Somchai. He is fifty-eight. He played tennis every weekend. Now he has high blood pressure. His tennis friends play too hard, so he is alone. His watch says one hundred twenty-eight, and he doesn’t trust what it means. So nothing changes. This is common: ninety-three percent of GPs in one survey prescribe exercise, only fifteen percent write it down. One guideline for everyone. No plan for Somchai. |
| **Like Netflix, for a safe and happy active life** | 1:00–1:15 | 49 | about 40 words). Heartstep works like Netflix for a healthy heart. The doctor signs a care plan once. Our recommender ranks a hundred activities and real groups by what you enjoy, what people like you kept doing, and what is near you. And our chat coach helps you start. |
| **Live demo: tennis is out, badminton is in** | 1:15–1:55 | 89 | about 90 words). Switch to the live demo, or let this recording play. Here is Somchai’s message. First, rules check for warning symptoms. Clear. The agent reads his signed limits from FHIR and the guideline from IRIS Vector Search. It filters a hundred activities down to sixty-seven. Tennis is out, too intense. A nearest-neighbour match finds what is closest to tennis: badminton, bowling, table tennis. It finds groups near his home, checks what similar users kept doing, and asks him to choose. His week is saved as FHIR Tasks. |
| **Built on IRIS for Health, with safety by construction** | 1:55–2:20 | 63 | about 60 words). Under the hood, everything lives in InterSystems IRIS for Health.
