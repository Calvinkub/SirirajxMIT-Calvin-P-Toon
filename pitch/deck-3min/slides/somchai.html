0:15–1:00 (about 100 words). Meet Somchai. He is fifty-eight. He played tennis every weekend. Now he has high blood pressure, and his doctor says: keep exercising, but gently. Then he goes home. His watch is full of data, but nobody tells him what is safe tomorrow morning. This gap is common. In one survey, ninety-three percent of GPs prescribed exercise, but only fifteen percent wrote it down. And in one Thai hospital, only twelve and a half percent of bypass patients came back for outpatient rehab. Most patients are on their own. They need a plan that fits their limits and their life.
