0:15–1:00 (about 105 words). Meet Somchai. He is fifty-eight. He played tennis every weekend. Now he has high blood pressure. His doctor gives him the guideline: thirty minutes, five days a week, moderate intensity. The same sheet everyone gets. At home, he has no clue what moderate means for him. His tennis friends play too hard, so he is alone. His watch says one hundred twenty-eight, and he doesn’t trust what it means. So nothing changes. This is common: ninety-three percent of GPs in one survey prescribe exercise, only fifteen percent write it down. One guideline for everyone. No plan for Somchai.
