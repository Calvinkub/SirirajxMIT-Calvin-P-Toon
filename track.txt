Cardiovascular diseases extend beyond the heart, they include diseases of the blood vessels in the brain (stroke), and in the limbs (peripheral artery disease). Affecting over half a billion people globally and more than one-third of Thais, cardiovascular disease remains the world’s leading cause of death, claiming nearly 20 million lives each year. In Thailand, it is the second leading cause of death, responsible for around 80,000 deaths annually.



From emergency care to lifelong prevention, cardiovascular disease demands innovation across every stage of care. How can we detect risk earlier, respond faster, and move closer to the future where cardiovascular disease can be prevented before it begins ?



It’s time to reimagine cardiovascular care and bring your ideas to life, shaping the future of prevention and treatment.
