# Pitch claims check (Draft 1 script)

| Claim in script | Status | Evidence found |
|---|---|---|
| "Up to 80% of doctors recommend exercise, only 15% give detailed guidance" | **Partly supported, wrong framing** | Frontiers Sports Act. Living 2026 review (PMC12990137) cites an Italian GP survey: 93% of GPs regularly prescribe exercise, only 15% give recommendations in writing, and only 24% perform or prescribe physical-capacity evaluations. Use "93% / 15%, Italian GPs" and name the source. A separate cardiologist survey (found by web search, not read in full) reported about 81% prescribing and about 11% in writing; treat that as a different study and do not mix the two sets of numbers. |
| "5-minute consultation" | **Unsupported** | No source found. Rephrase, or get a Siriraj number from the mentor. |
| "CPET is the precise data source" | **Supported, with a catch** | The same review says CPET is "significantly underutilized" (cost, training, equipment). Guidelines accept alternatives (see guidelines/app-parameters-qa.md §1). |
| "Peer communities improve adherence" | **Plausibly supported** | Thai cohorts: social support was the strongest reported predictor of readmission. Not a trial of peer groups. |
| "Proven tool to reduce readmission" | **Not supported for us** | Vendor data (Movn) claims 20–30%. We have no data. Say "designed to improve rehab adherence". |
| "Insurers pay for rehab packages" | **Unverified in Thailand** | Plausible via critical-illness riders. Needs insurer conversations. |
| "Premium from 499" | **Unvalidated** | Within the suggested basic tier (299–599 THB/month), but no Thai willingness-to-pay data. |
