## Part 1: The Problem

"Imagine you have just had a heart attack. Your doctor tells you that regular, safe exercise is one of the best things you can do to recover. Then you go home, and there is nobody to turn that advice into what you should do tomorrow morning.

This is a real gap. In an Italian survey of general practitioners, 93% regularly prescribed physical activity, but only 15% gave recommendations in writing [source: Komici et al., Frontiers in Sports and Active Living, 2026, PMC12990137]. In Thailand, after bypass surgery, one study found only 12.5% attended outpatient cardiac rehabilitation, and 83% said no one had told them about it [single-site study; source: PMC11456183].

Cardiac rehabilitation works, but most patients never get it. How do we help a patient, safely, between clinic visits?"

## Part 2: The Solution

"Our app helps patients follow the exercise limits their own clinician sets. It does not replace the doctor or the rehab team.

A clinician or rehab team confirms the patient's safe limits. From those limits and the patient's smartwatch data, an AI coach suggests one or two small daily missions, such as a ten-minute easy walk, at a time the patient chooses. The AI can only choose inside the clinician's limits. It cannot raise them, and it does not give medication advice.

If a patient reports chest pain, breathlessness, or dizziness, the app does not coach. It tells them to stop and get emergency help, using fixed rules, not AI.

We add family and peer support, because in Thai studies social support was the strongest reported predictor of hospital readmission [source and design caveat needed; correlation only].

We turn a clinician's plan into small, safe daily habits, so heart patients can rebuild their lives with confidence."

## Part 3: Business Plan

"We have two channels.

First, insurers. Critical-illness cover pays out after a heart attack, so insurers care about recovery. We offer our app as a recovery-support benefit for policyholders. We are designed to improve rehab adherence, which research links to fewer readmissions. We will prove our own effect in a pilot with pre-agreed outcomes [pilot design needed].

Second, patients directly. Patients with a clinician-confirmed limit start with a one-week starter plan. If they want continuous adaptation and tracking, they can upgrade to Premium: 1-month, 3-month, and 1-year plans, starting at [499 THB, price not yet tested with patients].

Programs like Movn Health in the US and Cardihab in Australia deliver digital cardiac rehab through clinicians. To our knowledge, we are the first Thai-language, patient-first, daily-mission coach built around the patient's own clinician limits [to confirm with a competitor scan]."
