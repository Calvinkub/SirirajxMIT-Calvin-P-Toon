How the numbers are built. Token load per active patient per day is an estimate: one daily mission call, two short voice check-ins, and a weekly clinician summary, about 234 thousand input and 32 thousand output tokens a month. On a frontier API that is under two US dollars a month. Self-hosting has a fixed GPU bill of about 2,110 US dollars a month for one H100 running all the time, so it only wins at scale, around 2,700 patients against Sonnet 5.5. We self-host for data residency and Thai speech, not because it is cheaper at pilot size. Capacity per GPU is not measured yet; that is what the next slide is about.
