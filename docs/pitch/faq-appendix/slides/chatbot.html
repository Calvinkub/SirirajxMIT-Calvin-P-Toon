Be fair: general models are strong reasoners, and both OpenAI and Anthropic now connect health records and Apple Health for US users. Our difference is not a smarter model. It is that the model is bounded: limits come from a signed record, safety is decided by rules before any model runs, the app acts first instead of waiting for a question, it works in Thai, and the care team is in the loop.
