# Pitch script — Draft 1 (verbatim from the team, 2026-10-17)

## Part 1: The Problem

"Imagine being diagnosed with a chronic cardiovascular condition, like recovering from a myocardial infarction. Your doctor tells you that modifying your lifestyle and exercising regularly is vital to your survival. But once you leave the clinic, reality sets in: generic advice is hard to follow, and building a safe exercise routine on your own feels overwhelming.

On the medical side, doctors face a massive constraint. Up to 80% of doctors recommend exercise, but only 15% provide detailed guidance. Why? Because a standard 5-minute consultation is nowhere near enough time to redesign a patient's entire lifestyle.

This creates a critical gap: cardiac patients need a safe, tailored 'exercise prescription,' but doctors lack the time to build one. How do we create precise exercise plans tailored to each individual's unique health context?"

## Part 2: The Solution

"Our solution bridges this gap with an Agentic AI application that delivers personalized, safe exercise and lifestyle planning for cardiovascular patients.

Instead of generic workout plans, our app analyzes deep individual context—combining a patient's cardiac, pulmonary, and metabolic risk factors, daily symptoms, lifestyle habits, and precise clinical data from Cardiopulmonary Exercise Testing (CPET).

Powered by Agentic AI, the platform designs an optimal, exact plan that adapts to complex health conditions with ease. To ensure adherence, we also connect users to peer communities with shared physical activity goals.

We turn complex diagnostic data into safe, actionable daily habits—giving heart patients the guidance and confidence to rebuild their lives."

## Part 3: Business Plan

"To scale this solution sustainably, our commercial strategy leverages two complementary channels: direct-to-consumer B2C and strategic B2B2C partnerships.

In our B2B2C channel, we sell directly to insurance companies who offer our platform as a specialized rehabilitation package for their policyholders. This provides insurers with a proven tool to reduce readmission rates and lower overall long-term care costs.

For our B2C channel, we drive adoption through a Freemium funnel. New users get a free, one-time 1-week schedule to experience the value of AI-powered exercise guidance firsthand. To unlock continuous adaptation and ongoing tracking, patients upgrade to our Premium subscription, available in flexible 1-month, 3-month, and 1-year plans starting at 499.

This dual approach combines institutional distribution through insurers with predictable recurring subscription revenue directly from patients."
